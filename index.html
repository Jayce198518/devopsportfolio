Approach

How I Work

01
Design & Validate

I begin by modelling the target architecture — network topology, identity boundaries, and failure modes — before writing a single line of provisioning code. Prototyping locally with Docker, kind, and WSL2 lets me catch configuration drift and dependency issues early, long before cloud resources are spun up.

02
Automate & Secure

Infrastructure is codified in Terraform, application state is managed through Ansible or Helm, and pipelines enforce quality gates before anything reaches a cluster. Secrets stay out of repositories, IAM follows least-privilege principles, and every deployment is traceable from commit to runtime.

03
Observe & Iterate

Production readiness means more than green health checks. I instrument services with Prometheus metrics, Grafana dashboards, and distributed tracing so anomalies surface before they escalate. Post-deployment feedback loops inform the next iteration, turning operational data into architectural improvements.

About Me

I am a platform-focused engineer who treats infrastructure as a product. My work centres on building self-healing, observable systems across AWS, Azure, and GCP using Terraform, Kubernetes, and modern CI/CD practices. I actively leverage agentic AI tools — Claude Code, Cursor, and OpenAI APIs — to accelerate infrastructure delivery, automate repetitive provisioning tasks, and embed intelligent security review into pipelines. I have shipped containerised workloads, automated provisioning pipelines, and security-hardened deployments that run reliably in production.
